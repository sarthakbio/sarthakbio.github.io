Research

Current

Focused ultrasound for T-cell activation

Mapping acoustic pressure, duty cycle and exposure time against T-cell activation, with flow cytometry and an EGFP-NFAT reporter, toward gentler, controllable CAR-T manufacturing.

Devices

Microfluidic cell isolation

Herringbone and size-plus-electrical-force devices for label-free T-cell, neuroblastoma and stem-cell separation, designed in COMSOL and validated on patient-derived samples.

Delivery

Lipid nanoparticle mixers

Parametric design of microfluidic LNP mixing architectures for nucleic-acid delivery, from geometry modeling to fabrication-ready CAD.
