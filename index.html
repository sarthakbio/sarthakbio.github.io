Biomedical Engineer · T cells & Cancer Mechanotherapy

Using physical forces to tune T cells against cancer.

I'm a biomedical engineer and Master of Bioengineering candidate at Rice University, working at the intersection of microfluidics, applied physics and cellular immunotherapy. In Dr. Michael King's lab I study how focused ultrasound, a form of mechanotherapy, can activate T cells for CAR-T cancer immunotherapy. Long term, I want to lead an academic lab in bioengineering.

King Lab · 2026

CRISPR knock-in NFAT reporter line

Stable EGFP-NFAT reporter at AAVS1 in Jurkat T cells, replacing transient expression for long-term, low-variability readout of ultrasound-driven NFAT translocation. Primers designed within the 60-nt oligo limit.

Imaging · 2026

FUS activation imaging pipeline

Python pipeline that segments nuclei and cytoplasm in CZI time-lapse stacks and reports NFAT-GFP translocation ratios for FUS-stimulated Jurkat cells, including a fix for mislabeled CZI channel metadata.

Imaging · 2026

Confocal-on-widefield overlay tool

Registration, thresholding and rendering of paired Zeiss confocal and brightfield files into publication-quality composites for liposome and nanoparticle binding experiments.
